About
Claude Usage Monitor tracks your Claude Code usage from claude.ai/settings/usage and displays it as a badge on the extension icon for quick visibility.
Visit claude.ai/settings/usage while logged in to see your usage data. The extension will automatically extract and display your current session percentage.
